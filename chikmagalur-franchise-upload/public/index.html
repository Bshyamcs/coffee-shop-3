Good To Know

Frequently Asked

How much investment is needed?

It depends on the model, location and size. The models above show an indicative range; our team shares a detailed breakdown during the discovery call.

Do I need prior experience?

Tell us about your background in the application. We provide barista training and operational support, and will discuss what suits you best.

Can I propose my own location?

Yes. Mention it in the application (you can also say you are still looking) and we will review the site with you.

How long does it take to open?

Timelines depend on the location and fit-out. We will give you a realistic schedule after the location review.

How do I track my application?

Sign in with your Google account (using the same email you applied with) or create an account. Your status and notes from our team appear under My Account.
